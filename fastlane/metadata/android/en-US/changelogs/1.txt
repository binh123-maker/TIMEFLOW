Initial release of TIMEFLOW:
- Offline-first Task CRUD backed by local SQLite database.
- Real-time Lockscreen Ongoing Schedule Notification.
- Compact (2x1) and Expanded (4x2) Android Home Screen Widgets.
- Exact task reminder notifications with reboot recovery.
- Calendar monthly planning grid with task density dots.
- Material 3 Light and Dark themes with offline Inter typography.
- Fully open-source under GNU GPLv3 with zero trackers and zero ads.
